Slide 2: The Hook / Vision

• Slide Title: Our Core Vision
• Bullet Points:
	• Transforming Kenya's agricultural supply chain from a source of waste to a pillar of wealth.
	• Building a zero-waste bridge between diligent smallholder farmers and modern commercial buyers.
	• Key Metaphor: Fast, fresh, and direct—from the farm to the table.

Slide 3: The Problem

• Slide Title: The Post-Harvest Crisis
• Bullet Points:
	• Massive Waste: Up to 40% of all food produced in Kenya spoils or is lost before ever reaching a consumer's plate.
	• No Infrastructure: Smallholder farmers lack access to localized, affordable, and functional cold storage.
	• Exploitative Networks: Farmers face extreme reliance on predatory middleman brokers, completely stripping their profitability.
	• Data Point: Research in Kiambu highlights market-access gaps and poor post-harvest handling as the primary drivers of economic loss.

Slide 4: The Solution

• Slide Title: Presenting FreshLink Kenya
• Bullet Points:
	• The Ecosystem: An end-to-end platform that seamlessly unifies a digital marketplace, physical storage, and smart logistics.
	• Community Storage: Proximity-based, solar-powered cold hubs built directly near farming clusters.
	• Direct Trade: Disintermediating the supply chain by connecting growers directly with institutional buyers.
	• Data-Driven: Providing smallholders with real-time analytics regarding market demand and fair pricing.

Slide 5: How It Works

• Slide Title: The 6-Step FreshLink Cycle
• Bullet Points (Sequential Steps):
	1. Harvest: Smallholder farmer harvests fresh, perishable produce.
	2. List: Farmer lists the available yield on the intuitive FreshLink mobile portal.
	3. Store: Produce is instantly moved to a nearby solar-powered FreshLink cold hub.
	4. Order: Commercial buyers browse real-time inventory and place automated orders.
	5. Deliver: FreshLink coordinates optimized collection and fast-tracked shipping.
	6. Payout: Digital escrow clears, and the farmer receives immediate, transparent payment.

Slide 6: Target Market & Opportunity

• Slide Title: Unlocking a Massive Market
• Bullet Points:
	• The Supply Side: Hundreds of thousands of Kenyan smallholder horticultural farmers growing high-value, highly perishable fruits and vegetables.
	• The Demand Side: Commercial institutions requiring a predictable, high-quality supply chain (Supermarkets, Restaurants, Hotels, Schools, and Wholesale Hubs).
	• Geographic Launchpad: Initial operations targeting high-yield horticultural counties directly supplying the massive Nairobi metropolitan area.

Slide 7: Business Model

• Slide Title: Monetization & Revenue Streams
• Bullet Points:
	• Storage Fees: Affordable pay-per-use micro-fees for community cold hub utilization.
	• Transaction Commission: A flat 5% marketplace commission taken on successful farmer-to-buyer sales (e.g., KSh 500 revenue on a KSh 10,000 transaction).
	• Logistics & Delivery Fees: Distance-calculated shipping fees paid during order fulfillment.
	• B2B Premium Subscriptions: Monthly recurring tiers for large enterprise buyers unlocking priority access to prime inventory.

Slide 8: Competitive Advantage

• Slide Title: Why FreshLink Wins
